## Who we are

**Serenity Circle+** is a virtual individual therapy and counselling practice in Kenya, focused on making professional emotional support more accessible, flexible, confidential and client-centred.

**Tagline:** Heal, Grow and Thrive.

## Our story

Serenity Circle+ was born from a simple truth: seeking therapy shouldn't mean putting your life on hold. For many people, getting support can feel complicated, expensive or just hard to fit into a busy life.

Common barriers include:

- Cost
- Busy schedules
- Work and academic commitments
- Distance or transportation
- Wanting the privacy of your own environment
- Feeling uncomfortable in a traditional clinical setting
- Not knowing where to begin

Serenity Circle+ exists to make mental health support easier to reach, so people can get the help they need without completely rearranging their lives.

> Support should fit into your life, not turn it upside down.

## Why Serenity Circle+ exists

We believe mental health support should be accessible, personal and built around the individual. Our aim is to create a space where you can:

- Talk openly
- Feel heard
- Explore what you're experiencing
- Understand yourself better
- Develop healthier ways of coping
- Work toward goals that matter to you

Healing and growth look different for everyone. There's no single path, and you're not expected to move through it at the same speed.

## Services

Serenity Circle+ offers **virtual individual therapy**: one-to-one sessions by video call, each a fixed 45 minutes, for both adults and children. There are no in-person, couples, family or group sessions.

| Service | Summary |
|---|---|
| Child Therapy | Helping young people build confidence, manage emotions and develop healthy coping skills. For ages 3 to 18, with a parent's or guardian's consent. |
| Education | Helping children navigate school. |
| Anxiety & ADHD | Personalised support to manage anxiety and the challenges of ADHD. |
| Stress & Depression | Practical tools to manage stress, lift low mood and restore balance. |
| Trauma & PTSD | Specialised trauma therapy to heal from past experiences. |

You do not need to have everything figured out before seeking support.

## How it works

1. **Book a session** — choose a suitable time and schedule your appointment.
2. **Start the conversation** — meet in a comfortable and confidential environment.
3. **Explore what matters** — discuss what you're experiencing, what brought you to therapy and what you hope to work on.
4. **Work at your pace** — develop strategies, insights and goals that support your individual journey.

Therapy isn't always linear — and that's okay. Every person's journey is unique.
